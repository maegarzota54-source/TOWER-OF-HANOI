How to Play

🎯 Goal

Move the whole stack of disks from the left peg to the right peg.

📜 Rules

  1. Move only one disk at a time.
  2. You can only move the top disk of a peg.
  3. Never put a bigger disk on a smaller disk.

🔢 Minimum number of moves

With n disks you need at least 2ⁿ − 1 moves.

💡 Tips

  • Start with 3 disks, then try more.
  • The smallest disk moves every second turn.
  • With an odd number of disks, the smallest disk goes left → right → middle → left… With an even number, it goes left → middle → right → left…
  • Stuck? Press Hint in Play mode.
